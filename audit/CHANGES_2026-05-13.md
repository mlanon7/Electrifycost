## Where the audit was wrong (overrides)

1. **CA electricity rate.** Audit §1.21 claimed EIA-current was 28.9 c/kWh and the site's 32.5 c/kWh was 12% too high. WebSearch against current EIA/ChooseEnergy/EnergySage data shows CA Feb-2026 = 34.26 c/kWh, May-2026 ≈ 34 c/kWh. The site was actually too **low**, not too high. I updated CA to `34.0`.
2. **Panel calculator Quick-answer block.** Audit §2.6 said the panel calculator page is missing a Quick-answer block. It already has one (line 84-86: "Quick answer: a typical 100A→200A service upgrade runs $2,500–$5,000…"). No change made.
3. **`@astrojs/sitemap` upgrade.** Audit recommended option A (upgrade the plugin). The Linux sandbox can't install platform-specific dependencies; I went with option B (custom postbuild script) instead — lower risk, zero blast radius.
